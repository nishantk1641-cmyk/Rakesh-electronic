How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser or Acode.
2. Replace the placeholder images in /assets with real photos if the business gives permission.
3. Replace the email placeholder and add the verified phone/WhatsApp number.
4. Verify business name, address, services, products and opening hours before publishing.
5. For a client pitch, publish the demo on GitHub Pages and send the preview link.

Note:
This is a presentation/demo website. Product, service, contact and business claims should be verified with the business before public launch.
